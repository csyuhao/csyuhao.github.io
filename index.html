Hao Yu is an Associate Professor in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Central South University. His research focuses on the security and robustness of intelligent systems, with particular interests in trustworthy machine learning and adversarial learning.

Before joining Central South University, he conducted doctoral research in Computer Science and Technology at the National University of Defense Technology under the supervision of Prof. Xinwang Liu. He received his M.S. degree in Cyberspace Science and Technology from Beijing Institute of Technology in 2022, where he worked with Prof. Meng Shen, and his B.S. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Inner Mongolia University in 2019.

His work has appeared in venues including NDSS, ICML, ACM MM, IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security (TIFS), IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering (TKDE), and IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing (TDSC).

💻 Research

My research examines the security and robustness of intelligent systems under adversarial and unreliable conditions. I focus on graph learning, large models, and multimodal representation learning, combining vulnerability analysis with methods for more reliable learning and evaluation.

  • Graph Neural Network Security: Studying how adversarial manipulation and backdoor attacks affect graph learning, and developing methods to detect threats and improve model robustness.
  • Large Model Security: Investigating the security of large models, particularly vision-language models, through adversarial prompting, jailbreak analysis, and robustness evaluation.
  • Security of Multimodal Representation Learning Algorithms: Examining how adversarial perturbations, incomplete views, and cross-view inconsistencies affect multimodal learning and clustering, and designing more robust representation and alignment methods.
