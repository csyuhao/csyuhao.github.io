Hao Yu is an Associate Professor in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Central South University. His research focuses on trustworthy machine learning, with an emphasis on adversarial robustness, backdoor attacks and defenses, and the security of graph neural networks and large language models.

He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from the National University of Defense Technology in 2026, under the supervision of Prof. Xinwang Liu. He received his M.S. in Cyberspace Science and Technology from Beijing Institute of Technology in 2022, where he worked with Prof. Meng Shen, and his B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Inner Mongolia University in 2019.

His work has appeared in venues including NDSS, ICML, ACM MM, IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security (TIFS), IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering (TKDE), and IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing (TDSC).

💻 Research

His research investigates vulnerabilities in machine learning models and develops methods to improve their security and robustness. His current interests span three areas:

  • Graph Neural Network Security: Analyzing adversarial attacks and backdoor attacks on graph neural networks (GNNs), and developing detection and defense methods to improve robustness.
  • Large Language Model Security: Investigating jailbreak attacks and adversarial robustness in large language models (LLMs) and vision-language models (VLMs), including attacks that combine textual and visual prompts.
  • Robust Multimodal and Multi-View Learning: Developing robust methods for representation learning and clustering with multimodal and multi-view data, with an emphasis on adversarial perturbations, missing views, and cross-view sample alignment.
